# Navigation 3D meshes

The GLB files in this directory are web-optimised derivatives of the editable
FBX meshes from **3Dicons v1** by Vijay Verma (`realvjy`). The source models
were created in Blender and published under the
[CC0 1.0 Universal dedication](https://github.com/realvjy/3dicons/blob/develop/LICENSE).

Official source and download references:

- <https://3dicons.co/about>
- <https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2022/05/3dicons-open-source-library-case-study-download/>
- <https://github.com/realvjy/3dicons>

The selected FBX files were converted to glTF 2.0 GLB, welded, simplified for
their 34px display size, and quantised. No raster textures are embedded.
