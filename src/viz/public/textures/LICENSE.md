# Timeline sand material

`timeline-sand-diffuse.webp` and `timeline-sand-normal.png` are resized,
bundle-optimised derivatives of Poly Haven's **Sand 03** material.

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_03
- Author: Charlotte Baglioni
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Source maps: 1K Diffuse JPG and 1K Normal (OpenGL) JPG

The diffuse derivative is converted to greyscale because event identity comes
from Atoma's action accent colour, not from the source material's brown hue.
