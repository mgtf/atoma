## Participation in the commons

Atoma provides a shared-learning service. Every run can use the platform's
existing agent catalogue, learned recipes and trust history. What a run learns
can update that same catalogue and history for subsequent runs, including runs
by other organisations. Contributions are not subject to an individual approval
step before reuse. Learned recipes may include executable code.

By submitting material to the service, you authorise the operator to process it
to perform your runs and to extract, store, adapt and reuse learned recipes
within this shared service, including execution for other organisations. This
permission does not transfer ownership of your submitted material or grant
other organisations general access to your projects. Submit only material for
which you have the rights and permissions needed for this processing and reuse.

## Organisation data and administrative access

Projects, workspaces, run traces and retrieval corpora are scoped to their
organisation. The catalogue, recipes and trust counters are shared across the
instance. Do not submit confidential material that must never influence a
shared recipe: the service does not promise that learning removes every detail
of its inputs, and shared learning is part of the service's operation.

Authorised platform administrators can read across organisation boundaries for
operation and support. These reads are audited, with owner notifications through
the service's notification system. Repeated reads by the same administrator of
the same organisation share an hourly audit receipt; this is not one
notification for every poll or a promise of email delivery.

## Credentials, costs and use

You are responsible for managing your organisation's memberships and authorised
use of its credentials. Model usage uses your supplied provider keys or supported
personal subscriptions under the relevant provider's terms. The service does
not meter or rebill those provider charges. Concurrency limits restrict run
admission; they are not financial spending caps.

Do not attempt to access another organisation's scoped data, bypass execution
boundaries, misuse credentials, or submit material you are not entitled to use.
Review generated results before relying on, distributing or deploying them.
Generated results and learned recipes can contain errors; reuse and trust
history do not guarantee correctness or suitability.

## Retention and withdrawal

Finished run traces and workspaces are eligible for the operator's retention
job after 90 days. Current project seeds and unfinished publications are held.
Eligibility is not an exact deletion deadline. Run metadata, payer attribution
and the learning lifecycle integrity record are retained separately; removal of
run bytes does not remove that history or automatically retract shared learning.

Contact the operator about access, removal or privacy requests. Applicable
personal-data rights continue to apply; this description of technical retention
does not waive them. The operator's privacy notice must identify the controller,
purposes, recipients, retention and applicable request process for its deployment.
