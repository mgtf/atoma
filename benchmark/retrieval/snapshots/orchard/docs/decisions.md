# Collection decisions

## O-010 — archived

Status: superseded by O-011.
The pilot required 48 hours of notice and allowed 3 parcels per booking.
The pilot used email for carrier cancellation alerts.

## O-011 — accepted

Require 24 hours of notice and allow up to 5 parcels per booking.
Carrier cancellation alerts use SMS, replacing the pilot email behavior.
Requests received exactly at the notice threshold are eligible.
The cancellation deadline remains 12 hours before pickup.

## O-012 — draft

Status: draft; not approved for implementation.
The draft proposes Sunday pickups and a 2-hour express notice period.
