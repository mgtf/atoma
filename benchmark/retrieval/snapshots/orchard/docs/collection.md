# Collection policy

## Booking

A collection must be booked at least 24 hours before pickup.
One booking may contain at most 5 parcels.
The pickup window starts at 09:00 local time.
Local pickup times use the Europe/Paris time zone.

## Changes

A booking can be cancelled until 12 hours before pickup.
Changing the notice period must preserve the cancellation deadline.
When a pickup is cancelled by the carrier, notify the shop by SMS.

## Labels

The parcel label lookup key is orchard_collection_reference.
Damaged labels are replaced at the collection point, without a new booking.
