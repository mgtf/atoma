# Billing policy

## Accepted offers

The monthly subscription costs 1900 euro cents per seat.
The annual subscription costs 19000 euro cents per seat for twelve months.
The annual offer uses the configuration key stripe_price_annual_studio.
All offers are denominated in EUR.

## Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation stops the next renewal; access continues until the paid term ends.
The refund request window is 14 calendar days after the initial payment.
Changing the billing period must preserve that refund window.

## Payment failures

A failed payment receives three retries, spaced 48 hours apart.
The customer receives an email after the third failed retry.

## Rounding

Charges use integer cents. Round a computed fraction up to the next cent.
