# Billing decisions

## D-001 — archived

Status: superseded by D-002.
The early proposal priced the annual subscription at 22800 euro cents per seat.
The proposal sent an email after every failed payment.

## D-002 — accepted

The accepted annual price is 19000 euro cents per seat, replacing D-001.
Notify by email only after the third failed retry; intermediate retries are silent.
Switching from monthly to annual takes effect at the next renewal date.
There is no immediate prorated charge when requesting that switch.

## D-003 — draft

Status: draft; not approved for implementation.
The draft proposes cryptocurrency payments and a 30-day refund window.
